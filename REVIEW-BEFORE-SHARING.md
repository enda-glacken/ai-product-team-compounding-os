# Review before sharing

Use this before publishing the repo or anything you add to it.

All items begin unchecked. This checklist is a human review aid, not a certification. Repeat it after adding or changing content, and inspect the actual folder or archive being distributed.

- [ ] Only intended scaffold or independently approved content is included; no hidden files beyond the intended ignore file, history, caches, backups, links to local files, or unexpected attachments.
- [ ] No private organization or person names, account identifiers, tenant identifiers, customer details, confidential product plans, internal links, or private paths remain.
- [ ] No credentials, tokens, private keys, environment secrets, raw messages, recordings, telemetry, or restricted evidence are present.
- [ ] Referenced evidence is authorized for this audience; source permissions remain unchanged. If permission is uncertain, omit the material and reference.
- [ ] Examples, claims, numbers, comments, metadata, and review records have been checked. Nothing implies an approval or verified result that did not occur.
- [ ] Assistant prompts do not authorize external writes, tool execution, or permission changes on their own. The recipient will configure their own access controls.
- [ ] Copyright, employer obligations, third-party terms, license requirements, and redistribution authority have been reviewed by the appropriate human authority.
- [ ] The exact artifact revision and intended recipients are covered by the review below.

New generic wording and an automated scan are not proof of legal clearance or absence of confidential information. No open-source license is supplied. Do not assume employer-owned materials are cleared for redistribution.
