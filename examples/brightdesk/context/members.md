# Members (fictional product brain)

## Members book the evening before or before 09:00 on the day
- **Evidence:** booking timestamps, Jan–Jun (bookings analysis project, E3)
- **Confidence:** high
- **Reasoning:** 71% of bookings fall in those two windows. Rejected reading: "members plan a week ahead". Only 9% of bookings are made more than 2 days out.

## About 1 in 5 members have push notifications off
- **Evidence:** waitlist-auto-offer Q2 (22% at 3 pilot sites), E6, E7
- **Confidence:** medium (3 of 40 sites)
- **Reasoning:** Notification-led features that rely on push alone will miss a fifth of members. Email-only members still act, but more slowly (E7). Rejected reading: "push-off members don't want notifications at all". They accepted 38% of emailed offers.
- **Implication:** design notification-led features with a second channel from day one.
